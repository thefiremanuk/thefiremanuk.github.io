Monitoring

A signal from the panel can reach a phone, or a receiving centre, without someone standing next to it.

Last updated 4 October 2026.

NowLogIt Live

NowLogIt Live puts events from a compatible connected system next to the site, the recorded activity and the keyholder response. It can replace a speech dialler, or show a panel event on the site plans where that panel allows it. NowLogIt

Call for prices

EMCS

EMCS is an independent UK alarm receiving centre. It monitors fire alarms 24 hours a day, calls the keyholders and follows the agreed response. I can fit the signalling and arrange the monitoring. EMCS

Call for prices

Ajax owner alerts

When the fire alarm goes off, the Ajax app sends a push notification to the owner's phone. An SMS or a call can also be sent, where those are set up. This is a non-emergency alert. It does not replace calling 999, or a monitored alarm receiving centre.
