Questions people ask before they book

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No. Book a visit and book the next when it's needed.

Where do you actually cover?

If your town is not on the list above, drop us a message or give us a call.

The fire alarm panel is in fault tonight. What should I do?

Note what the panel says and give us a call. If the sounders are running, treat it as urgent and evacuate.

Will I get something in writing?

Yes. A report follows the visit.
